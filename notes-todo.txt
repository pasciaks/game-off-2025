Ideas to consider


🌊 Wave Modes — Expanded List

⚡ Heat Wave

🔥 “Letters overheat under pressure.”

After each turn, one random tile on the board “burns out” (turns black / unusable).

Only words containing a “HOT” letter (A, E, I, O, U) score double.

⚡ Shock Wave

⚡ “One move triggers another.”

When you place a word, adjacent letters (in 8 directions) vibrate and shift position by one space randomly (scrambling nearby tiles).

OR: A shock wave extends your word by one random letter from the rack automatically.

OR simpler: Every word that uses “Z”, “X”, or “Q” triggers a shock, rearranging the surrounding 3×3 area.

🧠 Brain Wave

🌀 (already clever — right-to-left or bottom-to-top)

🌈 Sine Wave

 Word length must follow a pattern — e.g., 3-letter → 5-letter → 7-letter → back to 3.

 Points oscillate — 2× multiplier on alternating turns.

🕳 Gravitational Wave

🌀 “Massive pull.”

Newly placed words pull surrounding letters inward (shift them one step closer).

Or: Words placed near the center gain bonus points, simulating gravitational focus.

🧊 Cold Wave

❄️ “Frozen tiles.”

Random letters on the board freeze and cannot be used or replaced for 1–2 turns.

If you thaw them by making a word containing “ICE”, “COLD”, etc., you get a small bonus.

🧬 Quantum Wave

⚛️ “Uncertainty rules.”

When you place a word, one letter in it randomly changes after placement (collapses to a different value).

Bonus points if your word still remains valid after the shift.

💀 Death Wave

☠️ “Decay spreads.”

Each turn, one tile disappears permanently, starting from the edges (like erosion).

Adds urgency: board shrinks over time.

🌪️ Shockwave Chain (Power-Up Idea)

Place a word that triggers a wave animation that spreads across the board, affecting tiles or stealing from other players.

🧩 Extra Bonus Themes / Variants

If you want to rotate “waves” every few hours or per match:

“Echo Wave” – every word also repeats its last letter onto an adjacent empty square automatically.

“Light Wave” – only letters with straight lines (A, E, F, H, I, K, L, M, N, T, V, W, X, Y, Z) can be used.

“Dark Wave” – only curved letters (B, C, D, G, J, O, P, Q, R, S, U).

“Brainstorm Wave” – each player can see the opponent’s next tile for 3 seconds before their turn.

“Radioactive Wave” – every word that touches a glowing tile doubles in points but removes those tiles afterward.
